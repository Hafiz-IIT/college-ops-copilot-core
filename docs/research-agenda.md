# Research agenda

## Central question
**Can explicit evidence and ownership rules reduce misrouting and incomplete-ticket forwarding in student administrative workflows?**

## Hypotheses
### H1
Required-field gates reduce incomplete tickets reaching downstream offices.

### H2
Unknown-category abstention is safer than forced classification.

### H3
Transparent rule routing offers a useful baseline before ML intent classification.

## Experiments
1. Generate synthetic ticket sets across fees, exams, certificates, and hostel requests.
2. Add paraphrased/ambiguous categories for a future classifier and compare against deterministic rules.
3. Model SLA breaches and explore priority routing in a future version.

## Metrics
- routing accuracy
- missing-field recall
- forced-classification rate
- unknown-category abstention
- SLA coverage
